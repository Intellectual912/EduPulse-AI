The Fundamental Prediction Dilemma in Learning Analytics

Predicting earlier (e.g. Day 14) maximizes the remaining semester for tutor coaching, but students have submitted virtually no coursework. Predicting later (e.g. Day 56) yields higher accuracy, but 25% of the course has already elapsed, making it harder to rescue failing students.

Data Quality Report & Integrity Verification

01

Windowed Assessments

Only assessments that were both due on/before Day 28 AND submitted on/before Day 28 are considered. No future exams or late grading leakage.

02

Exclusion Ledger

5,055 students who withdrew before day 28 are excluded from training rather than used as a predictor. Their records are logged in excluded_records.csv.

03

Forbidden Target Guards

final_result and target_at_risk are strictly shielded. assert_no_leakage() halts execution if any target column appears in features.
